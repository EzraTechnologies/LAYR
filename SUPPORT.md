# Support LAYR development

LAYR is a free, open-source touchscreen interface for Klipper-powered 3D printers, developed and maintained by **Ezra Technologies**.

We build LAYR to make controlling a 3D printer simpler, more intuitive, and more reliable. Development, testing on different displays and printers, documentation, and maintenance take considerable time and effort.

If LAYR improves your printing experience, saves you time, or helps your hobby or 3D-printing business, please consider supporting its continued development through **[GitHub Sponsors](https://github.com/sponsors/EzraTechnologies)**.

You can contribute once or choose a recurring amount. Contributions are entirely voluntary, and sponsorship does not provide exclusive features, guaranteed support, or preferential access to releases.

LAYR remains free and open-source under the [GNU GPLv3](LICENSE), whether you contribute financially or not.

Other valuable ways to help include filing helpful bug reports, improving documentation, contributing code, testing new releases, and telling other makers about LAYR.

Thank you for helping us keep LAYR accessible to the 3D-printing community.

— **Ezra Technologies / LAYR Development**
